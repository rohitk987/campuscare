Average precision is the area under the precision-recall curve, so it does not depend on the threshold.

Where the rule and CampusCare differ on the test term, at threshold 0.30 (the library default is 0.35): 10 pairs share a room, a category and a week but are different incidents; the rule merges all of them and CampusCare merges 6. 39 true duplicates escape the rule because they were filed from a neighbouring room, under another category or more than a week apart; CampusCare finds 26 of them.

## 3. Technician assignment: Hungarian versus greedy

| Method | Mean plan cost | Strictly cheaper plans |
|---|---|---|
| Greedy: highest priority first, cheapest free slot | 3.51 | n/a |
| Hungarian over capacity slots (CampusCare) | 3.33 | 317 of 500 |

500 random instances, 2 to 5 technicians (capacity 3) and 3 to 12 jobs, the same cost matrix for both. The Hungarian plan is optimal for that matrix, so it was never costlier; it lowered the mean plan cost by 5.0% and by up to 32.4% on a single instance.

## 4. Hotspot detection

One simulated 180-day term on the same campus: 180 background reports at random places and times (about one a day), 3 busy rooms with 12 reports each spread evenly over the term, and 6 planted bursts of 5 reports in one room within 7 days. A good detector returns the bursts and leaves the background and the steadily busy rooms alone. A burst counts as found when 3 or more of its reports share one cluster, and a busy room as flagged on the same terms; precision and recall are over burst reports. ε₂ = ∞ is plain spatial DBSCAN.
