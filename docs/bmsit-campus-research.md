## Location setup

The Trust facilities page links a Google Maps place marker at 13.1341876, 77.5693927. The current contact page's map viewport centre differs (13.13421, 77.567923). Neither proves the campus boundary, a reporting radius, individual building coordinates or girls' hostel coordinates. Do not enable location checks using a guessed radius or choose a large radius to accept nearby off-campus readings. Configure and test each boundary on site; leave unverified areas disabled.

## Reporting scope

Relevant categories include classroom technology, electrical faults, furniture, plumbing, drinking water, appliances, lifts and gym equipment. CampusCare collects foreground location only when reporting, suggests buildings, validates configured floor and room selections, requires camera evidence and sends each complaint for staff verification. Progress tracking remains available off campus.

The official website links a general grievance portal at student.bmsit.ac.in. CampusCare focuses on facility maintenance and its reporting controls; it should not claim BMSIT has no digital grievance service. Public sources were read without signing into the college portal, enrolling staff, or sending messages.
